Support

Contextia is built and run by an independent solo developer.

Have a question, found a bug, or want to request a feature? Email johnhuangyijun2002@gmail.com — every message is read personally, and I'll usually reply within a few days.

Getting started

  • How do I start reading something? From the reader, tap "Add Article" to paste text or import a URL, or "Upload File" for a PDF/DOCX. You can also try "AI Picks" (curated articles matched to your level) or "Native News" (articles in your own language, for immersion practice).
  • How does word lookup work? Tap any word or select a sentence while reading to get an instant AI explanation, then save it to your personal vocabulary notebook with one tap.
  • What languages can I learn? English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, and Spanish are currently supported as learning languages. The app's own interface can be shown in English, Chinese, or Korean.
  • Do I need my own AI key? Word/sentence analysis is powered by an AI provider (DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, or Gemini) — enter your own API key for that provider in Settings. A small number of free trial uses may also be available without one.

Account & data

  • Is my data private? Yes — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what's collected and how it's used.
  • How do I delete my account? Go to Settings in the app and use the account deletion option, or email the address above and it'll be handled manually.
  • I forgot my password. Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen to reset it by email.

Is the app free?

Yes — Contextia is free to use. See the Terms of Service for details.
