Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Contextia ("the tool") is a language-learning reading tool, built and run by an independent solo developer. This page explains what data we collect, how it's used, and what you can do about it. It applies to both the website and the iOS app.

What data we collect

  • Account info: your username and password (the password is stored encrypted — we can't see it in plain text either), your Google account ID and email if you sign in with Google, or your Apple ID's unique identifier and (if you choose to share it) your email if you sign in with Apple
  • Web version only: any AI provider API key you enter yourself (stored encrypted)
  • Articles you upload, paste, or import, and the vocabulary/sentence notes you save
  • Your AI feature call count and estimated cost, so you can see your own usage
  • Your learning activity: which days you read or reviewed, your review answers (knew it / didn't know it), and the reading level you set for each language — used for your streak, review schedule and learning stats

How your data is used

  • When you use an AI feature, the content it needs is sent to a third-party AI service to generate the result: the word or sentence you select (with nearby sentences for context) for word/sentence explanations; the article text and, when word selection prioritizes your vocabulary, up to 80 words from your vocabulary list for immersion reading; the article text (up to its first ~6000 characters) for the Comprehension Quiz; public news headlines plus your reading level for AI Picks; and, if you tap "Read now" on a recommended article, that article is fetched from its public source page and saved to your own private reading collection (with the source name and a link back to the original), and its text is sent to generate an AI summary shown below it (not generated for sources that don't permit it). Your username, email, password and other account details are never included. In the iOS app this goes to DeepSeek through our hosted service (free daily allowance). In the web version, if you add your own key in Settings, it goes instead to the provider you choose there (DeepSeek / OpenAI / Anthropic Claude / Google Gemini) or to any OpenAI-compatible relay address you enter. In the iOS app, we ask for your permission before the first time anything is sent. This is the only case where your content is sent to a third party.
  • Your reading level and learning language are used to choose which recommended articles (AI Picks) to show you, and immersion reading picks words from your own vocabulary list by default. This personalization happens only within your own account; it is not used for advertising and not shared with or combined with data from other companies.
  • If you're the owner account and have enabled Google Sheets sync, your saved vocab/notes are additionally synced to your own configured Google Sheet.
  • If you connect Notion in Settings, your saved vocab/notes are additionally sent to the Notion page you choose, using an access token Notion issues after you authorize the connection. We only store that token (encrypted) and the ID of the page you picked — we don't see or store anything else in your Notion workspace. Disconnecting in Settings deletes the stored token immediately.
  • Outside of those two cases, your data is not shared with any other third party for any purpose unrelated to the tool.

Advertising (iOS)

This version of the app does not show any ads, does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If ads are ever introduced, this page will be updated in advance, and the update will be noted under "Changes to this policy" below.

Notifications (iOS)

If you allow notifications, the iOS app schedules local reminders on your device (e.g. "you have N words due for review") based on data already on your device. These are scheduled entirely on-device — nothing about your notification content or schedule is sent to us or to Apple's push servers.

Data storage and security

  • Account passwords are stored salted and hashed; AI keys are stored encrypted — neither is stored in plain text
  • Data is stored in a Postgres database hosted on Supabase
  • Login sessions automatically expire after 30 days and require signing in again
  • On iOS, a small amount of data (your saved vocabulary and reading history) is cached locally on your device so recently-viewed data is still available offline; this cache lives only on your device and follows the same account-deletion rules as your server-side data

Your rights

  • You can change your username and password anytime in Settings
  • You can contact us to request deletion of your account and data, or to export your own data
  • We only collect the data our features actually need, and nothing more

Changes to this policy

The tool is currently free to use; its feature scope or pricing may change in the future, and we don't promise the service will remain free forever. Any significant changes to this policy will be posted on this page, with advance notice where reasonably possible.
